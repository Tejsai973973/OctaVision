Problem Statement

The Communication Void

Individuals with neurological disorders may fully understand language but lack the physical ability to produce speech. This creates a catastrophic barrier between their thoughts and the outside world.

Neurological Disorders

Conditions like Cerebral Palsy affecting motor control and coordination.

ALS (Lou Gehrig's)

Progressive damage to motor neurons leading to total speech loss.

Physical Trauma

Strokes or brain injuries that hinder speech production despite linguistic ability.

The Crisis:

"Communication is slow and requires significant effort. Wrong outputs can cause stress and create emergency risks. Existing tools like eye-tracking are physically exhausting and confusing."

Secondary Research

Neural Basis of Communication

Research shows that the brain has specialized mechanisms for interpreting gestures and non-verbal signals. Even when production is damaged, the "Give-Me" gestures and intent markers remain detectable via EEG.

Source: Frontiers in Psychology (2015)

High-Performance Neuroprosthesis

Translating neural signals from the speech motor cortex into text has reached speeds approaching natural conversation. This validates the possibility of using brain-computer interfaces for rapid assisted speech.

Source: Nature (2023)

CNN + LSTM Architecture

Convolutional Layers (Spatial)

Identifies local waveform shapes like oscillations and motor-intent spikes. It extracts the physical "signature" of the brainwave.

LSTM Layers (Temporal)

Maintains the sequence of thought. It ensures the model remembers how the signal evolves over time for accurate classification.

Future Scope

Hardware Ergonomics

Transitioning from prototype sensors to a 3D-printed, custom-fit headset using Dry Electrodes to eliminate the need for conductive gel, making the device comfortable for 24/7 use.

IoT & Wheelchair Integration

Expanding the neural command set beyond "YES/NO" to control smart home devices (Lights, Fans) and motorized wheelchairs, granting patients physical autonomy.

Transfer Learning

Implementing models that can generalize across different users with minimal calibration time, reducing the "training fatigue" for new patients.

Linguistic Expansion

Moving from binary intent to full-sentence predictive text using Large Language Models (LLMs) gated by neural triggers.
